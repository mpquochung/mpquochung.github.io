Hello, I'm Mai Phan Quoc Hung (Hung Mai) 👋

I am currently a Research Scientist at B0Labs @ N2TP Technology, as well as a Research Intern at Singapore Management University, under the supervision of Assistant Professor Bin Zhu. My research focuses on generative modelling and robotics, particularly their intersection in robot learning and, more recently, world-action models.

I graduated from National Economics University in 2026. Previously, I was a visiting scholar at Yuan Ze University in Taiwan, under the supervision of Assistant Professor Naeem Ul Islam, where I conduct my first research on Robotics. I began my academic research journey as a research assistant at NEU Codelabs, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Xuan-Lam Pham on natural language processing and artificial intelligence in education.

Open to PhD opportunities for the 2028 intake.
